# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, no dependencies (user choice). Must open by double-click and deploy to any static host. Admin/login/catalog management are explicitly out of scope for now; a future migration to a framework is possible.

## Users

- Primary: young customers (teens to ~30) from Cosenza and around, who already follow @novum_store_ on Instagram and want to see what's new, what it costs, where the shop is and when it's open, often on a phone.
- Secondary (now): the shop owner, who will be shown this site as a pitch demo in the shop.

## Product Purpose

Showcase website ("vetrina") for Novum Store Abbigliamento, a streetwear clothing shop in Cosenza. It must present the shop's style and new drops, make products and prices browsable, and drive visits to the shop and contact via WhatsApp/phone. Success: a visitor understands in seconds what the shop sells, sees pieces they want, and knows how to get them (message, then come in).

## Positioning

A physical streetwear shop with its own curated drops (rhinestone flame flannels, graphic tees, camo, wide denim), shot by and on its own community in front of its own storefront. The shop and its people are the brand.

## Operating Context

- Shop: Piazza Fausto e Luigi Gullo 22, Cosenza.
- Hours: Mon–Sat 10:00–13:00 / 16:00–20:00.
- Info: 349 390 0045 (WhatsApp/phone).
- Shipping: NOT offered on the site for now (owner decision, 2026-10-02). Do not mention shipping until confirmed.
- Instagram: @novum_store_ (~2.2k followers). Drops announced as "NEW DROP" reels.
- Shop closes in August for holidays (e.g. 7–26 Aug 2026).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Owner workflow (confirmed 2026-10-02): the owner only adds garments with photos, and handles everything else on WhatsApp. Garments live in `capi.js` (one line each). No reels, videos or other content to produce.

- Showcase only: no cart, no checkout, no accounts.
- Prices shown are DEMO placeholders, clearly labelled as such, to be replaced with real prices.
- Product names/categories are authored for the demo and must be confirmed by the owner.
- Undecided: domain, hosting, real catalog, brands carried, online sales, shipping.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "Novum Store", subtitle "Abbigliamento".
- Logo: white flowing script "Novum Store" with tracked small caps "ABBIGLIAMENTO", on black (img/ig/logo.jpg, 150px only; a vector/hi-res version is needed).
- Physical identity: black glass storefront with black awning, logo doormat, white interior walls, light grey wood-effect floor, dark shelving.
- User wants: professional and super clear to use, plus small "nerdy" easter-egg touches that wow without hurting usability.

## Evidence on Hand

- img/ig/ig01–ig12.jpg: 12 recent Instagram post covers (360–480×640), taken from the shop's public profile for the demo. Must be replaced with original/hi-res photos with the owner's approval before going live.
- No testimonials, press, reviews or sales figures: never fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Clarity first: what, how much, where, when, how to contact, always within one tap.
2. The shop is the brand: real place, real people, real pieces over generic fashion imagery.
3. Easter eggs are rewards, never obstacles: everything works without discovering them.
4. Phone-first: most visitors arrive from Instagram on mobile.
5. Honest demo: placeholders are labelled, nothing invented is presented as fact.
