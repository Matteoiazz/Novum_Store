# Surface: index.html (vetrina one-page)

Scope: public showcase page for Novum Store. Mode: Persuade. Audience: young streetwear customers from Cosenza arriving from Instagram, mostly on phones; first viewer is the shop owner (pitch demo). Action: come to the shop or message on WhatsApp (349 390 0045). Proof: real shop photos (img/ig), real address/hours. Prices are DEMO, labelled.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every drop is told like the shop's own "NEW DROP 1/2" reels: numbered, vertical, full-bleed, tap to advance. Refuses the default white product grid with hero banner.

OWN-WORLD: Black owns the surface, white is the only ink, concrete grey and oak-floor beige as quiet secondaries. Heavy condensed white caps with hard drop shadow (the reel overlay voice), the script logo for signature only, rhinestone dot-matrix as ornament. Reel progress segments, numbered "01/03" counters, hard two-frame cuts.

STORY: The visitor sees what just dropped and what it costs, scrubs the pieces, knows the shop is open now and closes in N minutes, finds address/hours/WhatsApp without searching, and taps to message or navigate.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: left 60% a huge "NEW DROP" headline with drop counter and the NOVUM rhinestone wordmark; right a 9:16 reel player (progress bars, piece name, demo price, prev/next tap zones). Top bar: logo, nav, live open/closed status, WhatsApp button. Mobile: reel fills the screen under a slim bar, primary WhatsApp CTA sticky bottom.

FORM: Drop Reel, position 4 of my ordered list, seed key 20459c95. Raises: live status instrument (six-pack), strass wordmark scattering under cursor (alphabet storm), hard cuts no easing (acetate), black total commitment (lobes), spray-tag mark on the CTA (zine), "out N days ago" stamp per drop (brine).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
